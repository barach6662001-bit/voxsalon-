Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers to the things prospective clients most often want to know before getting in touch. If your question is not here, ask us directly — we reply to every serious enquiry.

A member of our team reads your message and responds — usually within one working day — to arrange an initial discussion by telephone or video call. That conversation is confidential and without obligation. Its purpose is to understand what has happened and give you an honest first impression of whether, and how, we can help. Nothing is charged and nothing begins unless and until you instruct us in writing.
It depends on the matter, so we will not quote a figure before we understand yours. What we can promise is the order of events: first the initial discussion, then — if we believe we can help — a written proposal setting out the scope of work and the fee structure before you commit to anything. You will never discover a cost after the fact.
The essentials are: any contract or terms you agreed to, proof of payments made or received, and the correspondence between you and the other party — emails, letters and messages, in date order if possible. Do not worry about polishing anything; organising the record is part of our job. Our Knowledge section includes a full article on documents commonly required for an initial review.
An initial review is normally completed within a matter of days once we have the documents. Beyond that, timescales depend heavily on the other party: some matters resolve in weeks through correspondence, while others — particularly those involving institutions or formal processes — run for months. We will give you a realistic estimate for your specific matter at the assessment stage, and update it whenever circumstances change.
