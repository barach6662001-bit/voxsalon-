Nature of our services

Palladium Law Limited provides legal and dispute-resolution services of the kinds described on our Expertise page. Where a matter requires activities or expertise that must be carried out by an appropriately authorised specialist, we involve or refer to suitable specialist counsel, and we tell the client that this is being done and why.

The precise scope of services for any engagement — including who will carry out the work and on what terms — is set out in writing before the engagement begins, and any statement about status or authorisation relevant to your matter can be requested from us in writing at that stage.
