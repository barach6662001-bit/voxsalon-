Privacy Policy

How Palladium Law Limited collects, uses, stores and protects personal information.

This policy explains how Palladium Law Limited (company number 12855412, registered office: Fulford House, Newbold Terrace, Leamington Spa, England, CV32 4EA — “we”, “us”) handles personal information provided through this website and in the course of enquiries and client matters. We take this seriously: the confidentiality of the people who contact us is fundamental to how this practice works.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us:

  • Enquiry information — the name, email address, telephone number and message you submit through our contact form or send to us directly.
  • Matter information — documents and details you provide if we go on to discuss or handle a matter for you.

This website does not use advertising trackers, marketing pixels or third-party analytics services, and does not set cookies for those purposes.

2. How we use it

Your information is used solely to:

  • respond to your enquiry and conduct the initial discussion;
  • assess, and where instructed progress, your matter;
  • meet legal, regulatory and professional obligations that apply to us.

We do not use your information for marketing. We do not sell it, rent it, or pass it to advertising platforms, data brokers or social media services.

3. Legal bases

Where UK data protection law (including the UK General Data Protection Regulation) applies, we rely on the following legal bases: taking steps at your request prior to entering an engagement, and performing that engagement once agreed; compliance with legal obligations; and our legitimate interests in operating a legal practice, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

4. Who can access it

Information about an enquiry or matter is accessible only to the people at Palladium Law working on it, and — where a matter requires it and you have been informed — to specialist counsel or service providers engaged under obligations of confidentiality.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry information that does not lead to an engagement is retained only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry, after which it is deleted. Matter files are retained for as long as legal and professional obligations require, and then destroyed securely.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for inaccuracies to be corrected, request erasure where retention is no longer justified, and object to or ask us to restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on our Contact page. If you are dissatisfied with our handling of your information, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), the UK's supervisory authority.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information in our care, including access controls and secure storage. No method of transmission is entirely without risk; if you prefer to provide sensitive material by a particular channel, tell us and we will accommodate it where practicable.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page. Material changes affecting active clients are notified directly.
