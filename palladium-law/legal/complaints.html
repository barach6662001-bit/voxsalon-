Complaints Information

If something has gone wrong, we want to hear it from you first — and to put it right where we can.

Our commitment

We aim to give every client careful work and honest communication. When we fall short of that standard, we treat the complaint as information we need: it is investigated properly, answered in writing, and never held against the client or their matter.

How to complain

Raise your concern with the person responsible for your matter, or — if you would rather not, or the concern is about that person — submit it through our Contact page marked “Complaint”, or write to our registered office:

It helps if you set out what happened, when, and what outcome you are seeking — but a complaint does not need to be formally drafted to be taken seriously.

What happens next

  • Acknowledgement: we acknowledge your complaint in writing within 5 working days of receiving it.
  • Investigation: your complaint is reviewed by a senior person not primarily involved in the events complained of, wherever the size of the practice allows.
  • Response: you receive a full written response within 28 days. If the investigation genuinely needs longer, we tell you why and give you a revised date.
  • Resolution: where we have got something wrong, we say so, and we set out what we propose to do about it.

If you remain dissatisfied

If our final response does not resolve the matter to your satisfaction, you may have further avenues available to you depending on the nature of your matter and of the services provided. We will set out in our final response any external routes that apply to your specific complaint, together with the relevant time limits.

Complaints about your bill

Concerns about fees are handled under the same procedure and timescales. Raising a genuine dispute about a bill pauses our expectation of payment of the disputed part while the complaint is investigated.
