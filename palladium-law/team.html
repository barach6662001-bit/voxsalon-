Matters are handled by people, not pipelines.

Palladium Law is deliberately a small practice. Every matter has a named person responsible for it — someone who has read the file, knows the history and answers for the advice given.

How the Practice Is Structured

Three functions, one file, no hand-offs into the void.

Whoever you speak to at Palladium Law, these are the responsibilities behind your matter — and how they work together.

01

Legal Counsel

Counsel are responsible for the legal substance of every matter: assessing the merits, setting the strategy, and standing behind every letter and submission that leaves the practice. Where a matter calls for specialist expertise, counsel select and instruct the right external specialists — and remain accountable for the whole.

02

Case Analysts

Analysts do the painstaking work that outcomes are actually built on: reading every document, reconciling dates and figures, constructing the chronology and flagging the details that change a case. Nothing is summarised until it has first been understood.

03

Client Liaison

Your point of continuity from the first enquiry onwards. Liaison keeps you informed in plain language, makes sure your questions reach the right person quickly, and ensures you are never left wondering what is happening with your matter.

Standards

What our people are held to.

Competence before confidence

No one at Palladium Law advises beyond their competence. Matters requiring specialist expertise are referred to, or supported by, appropriate specialist counsel.

Accountability by name

Every engagement letter names the person responsible for your matter. You will always know who is answerable to you, and how to reach them.

Discretion as a habit

Client matters are discussed only with those who need to know, inside the practice and out. That habit does not relax after a matter closes.

Candour with clients

Our people are expected to deliver unwelcome news early and plainly. A difficult truth on Monday is worth more than a comfortable silence until Friday.
