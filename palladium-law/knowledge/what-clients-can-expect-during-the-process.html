Working With Us

What Clients Can Expect During the Process

Instructing a legal practice should not feel like handing your matter into a black box. This is how the process actually works, stage by stage — and what we ask of you in return.

Before anything begins

Nothing starts by accident. After the initial discussion and review, you receive a written proposal that sets out what we intend to do, in what order, and on what terms. Work begins only when you have accepted it. If at that stage you decide not to proceed — or we advise you not to — the conversation ends there, with no charge and no residue: what you told us remains confidential permanently.

Communication: the rhythm you can rely on

Once a matter is underway, three habits define how we communicate:

  • You hear from us at every material step. When a letter is sent, a response arrives, or a deadline is set, you are told — in plain language, with a copy where appropriate.
  • Silence is never a mystery. Disputes involve waiting: institutions have response windows, counterparties take time. When a quiet period is expected, we tell you in advance how long it should last and what happens when it ends.
  • Questions get answered by someone who knows your file. Your point of contact is a person working on your matter — not a call centre reading from a screen.

Decisions: always yours

Our role is to give you an honest assessment and a clear recommendation at every fork in the road. The decision itself — to press on, to accept an offer, to escalate, to stop — is always yours. We will tell you plainly what we would do in your position, and we will act on what you decide, but we do not decide for you and we do not apply pressure. An offer of settlement, for example, is always reported to you as received, together with our view of it.

Timescales: honest, therefore approximate

We give estimates, not promises, because the pace of a dispute is set partly by people who are not in the room: counterparties, institutions, formal processes. What we commit to is this — our own steps are taken promptly, deadlines that bind others are tracked and enforced, and whenever the expected timescale changes, you learn of it from us, with the reason, before you have to ask.

A well-run matter feels uneventful from the inside. The work is happening; the drama is optional.

What we ask of you

The process works best as a partnership. Our requests of clients are few but real:

  • Completeness over comfort. Tell us the whole story, including the parts that do not flatter your position. Surprises help the other side, never yours.
  • Reasonable responsiveness. Some steps need your input or signature. Responding within a few days keeps your matter moving at its natural pace.
  • No parallel initiatives. Contacting the counterparty independently while the matter runs can undo careful positioning. If you feel the urge to act, call us first.
  • Preserve everything. Keep documents, accounts and devices that hold relevant material intact until the matter is fully concluded.

How matters end

Matters conclude in different ways: a negotiated resolution, a decision in a formal process, or — sometimes — a considered decision to stop, because the remaining road is no longer worth its cost. Whichever it is, the conclusion is documented: what was achieved, what remains open, and what, if anything, should be monitored afterwards. Your file does not simply go quiet; it is closed properly, and you are told so.

And afterwards

Confidentiality does not expire when a matter closes. Your information is retained only as long as legal and professional obligations require, and our discretion about your affairs is permanent. Should the matter ever reopen — a counterparty resurfacing, a new development on old facts — the file, and the people who know it, are still here.
