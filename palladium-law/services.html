Expertise applied with judgement.

Six areas of practice, one consistent method: establish the facts, assess the merits honestly, and pursue the route that is proportionate to what is at stake.

01

Financial Disputes

Advice and representation where money has changed hands and something has gone wrong: services that were materially different from what was promised, obligations that were never delivered, balances that are disputed, or funds that have not been returned when they should have been.

When this applies

  • You paid for a product or service that was mis-described or never provided.
  • A provider is withholding money you are contractually entitled to receive.
  • You are in a disagreement over sums due under an arrangement or account.

How we work

We begin with a structured review of the documents and the timeline. From there we set out your realistic options — negotiation, formal complaint, pre-action correspondence or escalation — together with what each route involves, before anything is put in motion.

02

Banking & Payment Disputes

Structured assistance with disputed card payments and bank transfers, complaints to banks and payment institutions, and reasoned escalation where a legitimate claim has been rejected or ignored.

When this applies

  • A transaction was taken from your account that you dispute.
  • Your bank or payment provider has declined a claim you believe was properly made.
  • A complaint you raised has stalled without a substantive response.

How we work

Payment disputes are won and lost on precision: dates, references, terms and the exact sequence of events. We assemble that record properly, present the claim in the form the institution is required to consider, and manage the correspondence until a substantive answer is given. Where an internal process is exhausted, we advise candidly on whether further escalation is justified.

03

Contract & Commercial

Review, drafting and negotiation support for commercial agreements — and clear, professional intervention when a counterparty has failed to do what they agreed to do.

When this applies

  • You are about to sign an agreement and want to understand what it actually says.
  • A supplier, partner or client is in breach of their obligations.
  • You need firm correspondence that protects your position without inflaming it.

How we work

We read contracts the way disputes are later argued: clause by clause, with attention to what is missing as much as what is present. In live disagreements, our first aim is a commercial resolution that preserves value; our second is a properly protected position if resolution proves impossible.

04

Debt Recovery

Measured, professional recovery of sums owed to individuals and businesses — from initial letters through negotiated settlement, with escalation pursued only where the evidence and the economics justify it.

When this applies

  • Invoices remain unpaid despite reminders and promises.
  • A personal or business loan has not been repaid on the agreed terms.
  • You want the debt pursued firmly, but without damaging conduct in your name.

How we work

We verify the debt and the documents behind it, then open structured pre-action correspondence that sets out the claim and a clear route to payment. Many matters resolve at this stage. Where they do not, we advise honestly on next steps — including, where appropriate, that further pursuit is not economically sensible.

05

Civil Litigation Support

Preparation and management of civil claims: organising evidence, constructing the chronology, preparing instructions and coordinating specialist counsel where the matter requires expertise beyond our own.

When this applies

  • A dispute is heading towards formal proceedings and needs disciplined preparation.
  • You hold a large volume of documents and need them turned into a coherent case.
  • You require a single point of coordination between yourself and counsel.

How we work

Litigation rewards preparation above almost everything else. We build the file the way it will eventually be read — complete, ordered and honest about its weaker points — and keep you informed in plain language at each procedural stage.

06

Regulatory Complaints

Formal complaints to financial institutions and service providers, prepared to the standard the receiving organisation is obliged to take seriously — and advice on the further avenues that may be open to you if a complaint is not resolved.

When this applies

  • An institution has treated you unfairly and informal contact has achieved nothing.
  • You have received a final response you believe is wrong.
  • You are unsure which body, if any, is the right one to hear your complaint.

How we work

A well-founded complaint states the facts, the failing and the remedy sought — briefly and with evidence attached. We prepare complaints in exactly that form, track the deadlines that apply to the institution, and advise you realistically on the prospects of each further step before you take it.
