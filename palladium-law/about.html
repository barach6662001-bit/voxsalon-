Palladium Law Limited advises individuals and businesses on financial disputes and commercial matters. This page explains who we are, how we think, and what you can expect from us.

Who We Are

Palladium Law Limited is a private legal practice incorporated in England and Wales in September 2020. We act for private clients and businesses whose financial or commercial affairs have taken a difficult turn — a payment that should not have been taken, a service that was not what it claimed to be, a contract that a counterparty has failed to honour, or a sum of money that has simply not come back.

We are deliberately not a volume operation. Matters are taken on selectively, assessed honestly, and handled by the people who took responsibility for them at the outset. If we do not believe we can add genuine value to your situation, we will say so at the first conversation — clearly and without charge.

Much of our work arrives by referral and recommendation. That shapes how we behave: the reputation of this practice rests on how each individual matter is conducted, not on advertising.

What We Do

We bring structure to disputes that feel chaotic — establishing the facts, weighing the options and pursuing the sensible ones.

Financial & payment disputes

Disputed transactions, mis-described services, complaints to banks and payment institutions, and escalation where a legitimate claim has been rejected.

Contract & commercial matters

Review and negotiation of commercial agreements, and firm, professional correspondence when the other side has not done what they agreed to do.

Debt recovery

Recovery of sums owed through structured pre-action steps and negotiation — with proportionate escalation where it is justified by the evidence.

Litigation support

Careful preparation of civil claims: organising the evidence, building the chronology and coordinating specialist counsel where a matter requires it.

Our Principles

The standards we hold ourselves to.

01

Confidentiality

Everything you share with us is treated as confidential from your first message onwards — whether or not you ultimately instruct us. Your information is used to assess and progress your matter, and for nothing else.

02

Transparency

Before any engagement begins you will know, in writing, what we propose to do, how the work is structured and on what terms. Our company record is public and permanently open to your inspection at Companies House.

03

Candour

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they hope to hear. If a claim is weak, we say so. If circumstances change, you hear it from us first. We never quote success rates or promise results.

04

Proportionality

Escalation is a tool, not a reflex. We recommend the steps that are proportionate to what is at stake, and we will tell you when the sensible course is to settle — or to stop.

05

Care with your data

We collect only what a matter requires, hold it securely, and do not pass client information to marketing platforms or third-party services. Our Privacy Policy sets this out in full.

06

Professional responsibility

We act within our competence, decline matters that fall outside it, and involve specialist counsel where a matter genuinely requires expertise beyond our own. If something goes wrong, our complaints procedure is published on this site.
